# The deeds' fate

Agreed with Matt on 27 Oct: "they don't really do anything for the story ... I sell them 100% of the time as it's 5k
early cash. What can we do with the options we already have in terms of impact?" The three fates each get an early
cost and a late payoff, through systems already in the game. The bot never decides a fate, so its balance is unchanged.

| Fate | Now | The Bailiff (County) | The Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sold (at the Fence) | 5,000 tins at today's prices | Seizes your best piece, as before. When he's down, the deeds are in his case: they drop once, ever (`deeds_back`), and the choice opens again | Gone |
| Held (brought home) | Nothing | Seizes the deeds, not your kit: no cost to the fight. Get him under half and he lets go; if the run ends in his hands (fled, timed out, dead) he keeps them: sold, for good | Out of the pile and never stamped: property, not money. They sell at the full new price after it |
| Burned (ditched and left, or burned at home from the Kit room) | Nothing | His warrant is void: nothing seized | - |

- The Radio has a line for each fate (Kev, in the turn once it's decided). The Bailiff's reading is a line in the log
  (`Words.DEEDS`: void, seized, taken, back).
- Held deeds can still be sold or burned later. The Kit room's Burn asks twice.
- The deeds' pick says it on its third line: "Bricks, not money: the Issue can't touch them".
