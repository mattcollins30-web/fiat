# The tutorial: naked, one site

Agreed with Matt on 28 Oct: "change the order of things to give the player a tutorial as a 'naked' which is one level to
unlock everything including the poacher. This is where we can intro fight mechanics and the move system etc. Then go
into the existing game plus a poacher card intro." Design only until Matt signs it off; the canvas's "The tutorial".

## Matt's calls

- One site, staged beats: a short scripted run, each beat waiting for the player to do its thing.
- Naked has no class, so the move beat uses a borrowed move, tutorial only: Throw a brick.
- The Poacher finds you at the end and gets you out; home, his class card plays (the missing card) and he's the
  first runner.
- A first camp starts in the tutorial, everything shut until it's done; later camps offer Skip.

## The site

The garages behind the camp: the tutorial's alone, never on the Map. Kept kind: small gangs, low fire, HP stops at 1
before beat 8. While a line's up the clock and the gangs wait; only the beat's thing is lit.

## Throw a brick

The borrowed move: 20 energy, 8 s to come back, the one in front stunned 2 s. Gone when the Poacher takes over (his
Clean shot is the first of his).

## After

Home: the Poacher's card ("Off the hedge at the garages, the day you went out with nothing"; his two lines in
docs/voice/recording_list.md), then the Map, the Kit, Stats and Training open with their cards as now. What the
naked run found comes home with him.
