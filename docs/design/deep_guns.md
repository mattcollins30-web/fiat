# Deep bases (10 Oct 2026)

From Matt's playtest: the drops top out at the Bank - "a 100 dps SMG at the bank" and nothing better past it, so
the depths have no gun to chase. Agreed with Matt (10 Oct): **new deep bases; leave the campaign as it is**.
Board: Guns-D on the design canvas.

## 1. The bases

Each gun type gets three bases past the campaign's six tiers, found only at depth: tier 7 from site level 12, tier 8 from 16, tier 9 from 20 (the Barracks
at depth 4, 8 and 12; section 2). Each tier is about
+18% damage a second on the type's own line. Names are first goes - change any of them.

## 2. How they drop

- `Data.CAMPAIGN_TIER` 6 is the campaign's top; `Data.MAX_TIER` is 9. Every slot has bases to tier 9, so no roll
  meets an empty tier.
- A site's loot tier grows with its level (`DEEP_TIER_LEVELS` 12, 16, 20): the Barracks reaches 7 at depth 4, 8 at
  depth 8 and 9 at depth 12; the streets' long, gentle ladder needs more depth. The campaign's sites and their
  drops stay as they are, and a campaign site's "a tier up" (Local knowledge, a word, a cache, the ledger) stops at
  6 (`loot_cap`).
- The Fence's Arms dealer stocks deep bases at the runner's depth.
- Each base gets a line in `words.gd` and a value on the tier-6 line.
