## 1. Why play on

The pieces are there (docs/design/rebuild.md, 18 Oct: the town as the reason past the lorry; docs/design/the_issue.md,
25 Oct: the Issue as the money's turn). They don't read as a goal because:

- **Nobody says it.** The opener sets up a camp and tins; nothing says what they're for. The rebuild arrives as a tab
  on the Radio after the story, its places as stat lines ("+2 pack cells").
- **It isn't seen.** A rebuilt place changes a number, not the town. The Map looks the same with the substation on.
- **Money has no job.** Tins pile up (389k) because past the Fence and the Bench there's nothing to spend them on -
  which is the Issue's whole point, but the Issue is 20 h away.
- **The order's heads gate the rest.** The Lineman's key gates the mint, the mint the Issuer, the Issuer the County -
  all behind levels 35-45 with the XP slowing from 22.

### The proposal: the town is the goal, the Issue is the turn

1. **Said from the start.** Kev's opener (or the Manager's, once he's down) names it: the town ran on something; get it
   running and the Pitch stops being a squat. A one-line goal on the Map's header from then on ("The town: 3 of 6
   running"), the next step named.
2. **Seen.** Each rebuilt place lights up on the drawn Map (its block lit, a figure in the street, its own small art)
   and brings something human back to the Pitch (a stall, a face at the Fence, a bus that takes runners further).
3. **Money's job before the Issue.** The town costs tins too, not only materials: wages for who runs each place, and a
   rank's upkeep. Tins buy the town; the town is what tins were for. Then the Issue (the order prints its money) wipes
   that out - the act's turn, at about 6-7 h, the title paying off.
4. **The order's heads on the town's road, not past a level wall.** The Lineman, the Registrar and the Issuer come in
   turn as the town's places do, at levels the road reaches (the numbers plan's 30 / 34 / 38), the County after the
   Issue (its first zone at 40, not 50).

## 2. Signals worth playing

Matt's two points: they're thin, and they're a chore to start. Chaining fixes the chore; the rest makes a chain worth
running.

### The chain (Matt's call)

A **night on the air**: set off once and the signals come one after another.

- You pick a starting signal (or the top held one). Win, and the next comes straight in: the next held signal, then a
  fresh one rolled at the reach + 1 (its codes by your tuning rules), and so on.
- **No going home between.** HP, meds, rounds and the pack carry over; what's found piles up in the pack; no rest. The
  risk grows with every link, the haul with it.
- **Get out at any break** with everything (the way out between fights); a death loses the night's haul and the kit, as
  today. The odds shown at each break are for the next link as you stand now.
- **Tuning rules,** like the loot rules: the codes you want, the ones you won't take, the wagers you'll accept - set
  once, used for every link you didn't tune by hand.
- **It ends** when you get out, die, run out of the night's clock, or the pack's full.

Options for Matt:

- **A** - the chain above, open-ended (the night ends when you stop).
- **B** - a set length, chosen at the start (3 / 5 / 10 links), its haul multiplied at the end if you finish it.
- **C** - A with a bonus at every fifth link (a head, a stash, a fat drop).

Claude's pick: **C**. Open-ended keeps it "one more"; the fifth link gives a rhythm and a reason to push.

### What makes a signal worth it

- **Say what it drops** on the card: "a resist piece", "a unique", "fuses, cable drums". You pick (or tune) for what
  you need - which also answers "too hard to craft resists": a signal can bring them.
- **The town's needs on the air.** A rebuild's materials come mostly from signals named for them, so the goal in §1
  points at the Radio.
- **Heads and events on signals** - the run events (the Vagabond, the threats), a head away from home at the chain's
  fifth link, Kev's leads as chains of their own (a person, a stash, a rival crew).
- **Wagers redone** (playtest item 19) as a choice at the chain's breaks: take on harder terms for the next links for a
  better haul.
