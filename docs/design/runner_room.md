# The Runner room: the loadout (10 Oct 2026, Runner-R A)

Matt: the Runner is the worst-looking screen now; it hadn't changed since the first build. Agreed: **A, the
loadout**, with his order: the weapons, the charm and what's worn down the left; the helmet over the armour, then
the rig and the pack down the right. Board: Runner-R on the design canvas.

## Built

- A runner strip over the column (88 px): the face, the past and level, HP and its bar, damage a second, armour,
  pace, cells and pouches.
- The loadout (608 × 516): the figure in the middle on the painted kit room (art/places/the_kit_room.png at 40%,
  a vignette; the drawn light while it's missing). Seven cards of one size (198 × 119), glass, edged in the item's
  rarity: the slot named always, the item's name and its one figure (damage a second and rounds, armour, pouches,
  cells, a charm's rarity). A long gun lies across its card under the slot's name; the rest stand at the left. An
  empty Primary is edged red ("nothing to fight with"). The fourth place down the left is what's worn: its worth
  at the Fence and how many slots are filled.
- Drop onto a card fits it (its own slot only); press it to pick it; drag the picture off to the stash.
- The rig and the pack under it, as before.

## The figure: a brief for the redraw (art/body.png)

- **Canvas**: 180 × 488 px (or 90 × 244 drawn at 2x and scaled whole), transparent background. Drawn at its own
  pixels like the items; the game shows it 1:1, centred between the cards, top at 14 px.
- **Framing**: full length, standing, facing the viewer (a slight 3/4 turn at most). Head top about 8 px from the
  canvas top, boot soles about 4 px from the bottom. The whole silhouette, arms included, inside 170 px wide - the
  cards sit 9 px either side.
- **Pose**: neutral and still, weight on both feet, arms down and a little away from the body (a paper doll's,
  so it reads as "the body the kit goes on", not an action shot). Hands empty.
- **Who**: a generic runner, not a class - plain layered work clothes (a hoodie or knit jumper, a work jacket's
  absence is fine since the Armour card says what's worn), cargo trousers, worn boots, a beanie or bare head. No
  weapon, no bag, no helmet: the cards carry those. Grimy, patched, British post-collapse estate, not military.
- **Light**: cold light from above and slightly front (the room's cone): a cool highlight on the head and
  shoulders, the legs darker towards the boots. No cast shadow on the ground - the room draws its own.
- **Palette**: muted - charcoal, slate blue-grey, olive, mud brown, a desaturated skin tone; one darker outline
  (not pure black) so it lifts off the kit room's painting at 40%.
- **Style**: as the items and the people in art/ (chunky readable pixels, limited ramp per material, no
  anti-aliased soft gradients).

A later option, not built: a figure a class (art/body_<class>.png, the Drifter's beanie, the Doorman's jacket),
falling back to art/body.png.
