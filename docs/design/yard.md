# The Yard's buildings as lanes (9 Oct 2026)

From Matt's playtest: "I quickly become bored of the yard's upgrade paths and more importantly quickly unlocked
most of them before I'd unlocked the third zone - given the yard is the one constant it needs to be more
interesting and have more legs - I still need yard upgrades even when I'm 9 depths deep. ... you should just get
the ability to craft whatever on anything you like from the start of the game. Overall the choices need to be
more memorable and scale more."

Agreed with Matt (9 Oct): **lanes with picks**; **cash and deep salvage**; **the buildings come with the Yard
when it moves**.

Today: eight buildings, 2-3 levels each, £190k in all before inflation, no prerequisites; the bot buys each the
moment it can, and they are all done by hour 3-6, at or just after the City opens. A move resets them all.

## 1. The shape

- Each building is a **lane of six columns**, like the trees. Columns 1-5 hold **two picks; you take one**. The
  one not taken stays on show, dim, so the choice is remembered. Column 6 is the building's **rank**: one tile,
  bought again and again, its price climbing - the sink that never ends.
- A pick can be **changed** later ("refit") for half the column's price. A choice costs something to undo,
  not everything.
- **Columns open on the campaign and the depth**, not on cash alone, so the Yard keeps pace with the game:

| Column | Opens | Paid in (first figures; × inflation as every price) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | from the start | £1,000-2,500 |
| 2 | the Town open | £5,000-8,000 |
| 3 | the City open | £12,000-20,000 |
| 4 | the campaign done | £30,000-50,000 |
| 5 | depth 6 held on any site | £80,000 + 10 salvage |
| 6 ◆ rank | depth 9 held on any site | £50,000 × 1.25ⁿ + (5 + n) salvage, n ranks bought |

- **Salvage**: three new kinds in the purse (no cells), one a zone, dropping from kills on that zone's sites
  from depth 4 (1% a kill a depth past 3: 5% at depth 8), home with the run, lost with a death: **Copper** (the Estate), **Safe steel** (the Town), **Army surplus** (the
  City). Each building's column 5 and rank want one kind, spread so every zone stays worth running deep.
- **A move keeps every building** - picks and ranks. (Today a move resets them and the rebuy costs more with
  each yard as inflation climbs.)

## 2. Crafting open from the start

The Bench stops gating anything: every job, every mount, sockets, scrapping, building from scrap, any tier of
improvised gun, and the cache events (which wanted Bench 2) are there from the first minute. The Bench's lane
makes crafting **better**, not possible.

## 3. The lanes (as built, 9 Oct; Matt took the draft and layout A)

is open from the start (its first column halves the price, or a free word every fifth run); Two locks keeps the
best of the rest; the Stash's Racks, Bins and Annex, the Clinic's Stockroom and Drip, the Fence's Orders and
Insurance desk, the Radio's Long range and the Lookout's column 2-5 picks replace draft ones that duplicated
something already in the game.

Every one of today's levels is in a lane somewhere (the Stash's cells, the Clinic's rest and shelf, the Fence's
stock, the Radio's three, the Generator's hours and pouches, the Lookout's four, the Kennel's three); an old save takes the closest picks - see §5.

## 4. The screen

The Yard room becomes eight lanes, the trees' layout: a label block (the building, its rank, the next column's
gate), six columns of two picks, the rank tile alone in the sixth. The detail strip under them names the pick,
its other half, the price (cash and salvage) and Build / Refit. The purse's chips (cash, notes, scrap and the
three salvages) and Move the Yard sit in the header. Two layouts on the canvas: **Yard-L A** (all eight lanes,
compact) and **Yard-L B** (eight short lanes plus the selected building opened full width).

## 5. Saves and balance

- An older save's levels map to the matching picks (level 1 of the Stash = Shelving, and so on), free, side A.
  Where a level sat on the B side of an exclusive column (the Clinic's bigger shelf, the Lookout's sixty-run odds,
  the Kennel's quality) the save keeps the A side; a refit is half price.
- The balance pass at the end of the playtest list sets the prices; a gate: *the Yard still has something to
  buy at depth 9 on hour 16*, and *no building has its column 5 before the campaign is done*.
