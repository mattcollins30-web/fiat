# Fiat — the vision (draft, 21 Oct 2026, for Matt's OK)

*Let it be done.* An ARPG auto-battler. Mundane Studios.

This replaces "the idler cut" (docs/design/concept.md, which stays as the build's history). What we built isn't an
idler: the runs fight themselves, but the game is the build - the classes, their trees and elements, the gear chase
and the Bench - and an endgame that never ends.

## The pitch

Britain after the money stopped. You run a crew of five specialists out of the camp, each with their own level, tree,
element and kit. A run fights itself; your job is what goes in: which runner, built how, carrying what, against which
signal. The loot is Diablo's and Path of Exile's - tiered affixes, uniques, sets, sockets, a Bench to perfect a piece -
in a setting that's contemporary and British and never fantasy.

## The pillars

1. **The build is the game.** Five classes, two elements each, a tree per class, gear that's rolled for the build:
   damage all of one element, its increase, its dots; resists to the cap; HP and armour.
2. **The right runner for the fight.** Every signal shows its crew - their damage types, their resists, its codes -
   and the odds for each of your classes. Seventy per cent shock resist? Swap to the bleed build and walk it.
3. **The chase never ends.** Past the campaign the Radio's signals climb forever; their drops' item level follows the
   signal's, and gear scales with item level, so your reach sets your loot and your loot your reach.
4. **Risk is real.** A runner who dies goes back to level 1 with a trait, as today; their worn kit goes unless insured.
   The others are untouched.

## The loop

- **The campaign** (about 3-4 h by hand): the Outskirts, the Estate, the Town, the City, a head at the end of each;
  the Bank about 1.5-2.5 h. The rooms open as you go.
- **The Radio** (forever): papers make signals - faint, clear, full - each a site, a crew, a level round your reach and
  its codes. Beat one at or past your reach and the reach climbs.
- **Kev's story**: three beats on the Radio, as today, ending at the depot; its convoy is the lorry, once
  (about 10-13 h by hand). No move after it.
- **Paragon**: past level 50, every level is a paragon point, shared by every class, on a small board (find, tins,
  damage, HP, resists), with no top. It takes over what moving the Pitch used to give.

## The crew (replaces respec, retirement and the Pitch move)

- **The class screen swaps classes**, it doesn't respec. Each class keeps its own level, XP, tree, element and worn
  kit; a swap is instant, at camp, never on a run. A class you haven't played starts at level 1, and levels at the
  normal rate (no catch-up).
- **Death** puts that class back to level 1 with a trait, as today; the trait stays with the class.
- **Gone**: retirement and the wall's traits, the Pitch move and everything it gave or kept (find and tins a move,
  training points, notes and scrap carried, the stall's wait, the class reset). What a move gave goes to paragon; the
  Map's points stay yours for good.

## The stash

Real tabs: one shared by every class, and one or two of each class's own that the others don't see. The kinds (guns,
body, bags, meds, rounds, parts, uniques, sets) are sorts inside a tab.

## Gear scaling (the endgame is forever)

The crews grow about 8% a level in HP and in fire, compounding. Past the deep tier (level 20) a base's damage, armour
and HP grow with its item level at a little under that (7% first; 5% after the 21 Oct reads, an edge halving in about 10 levels), and a drop's item level is its signal's.
The gap is the chase: paragon, the Bench and the right class for the crew close it. Without this the reach walls at
35-45 whatever the Bench does (the 20 Oct reads).

## The gates (agreed 20 Oct, with the lorry folded into the story)

| Gate | Target (by hand) |
|---|---|
| The Bank | 1.5-2.5 h |
| The campaign | 3-4 h |
| Deaths | one run in 10-30, the first by about 2 h |
| Wounds | 5-30% of runs |
| Odds at the gate | median about 70-90% |
| Kev's story and the lorry | about 10-13 h |
| The endgame | the reach never stands more than about 1 h |
| Signals | faint about 90% won; full at the reach about a coin flip |
| Builds | every class's elements finish; plain builds just finish |

- Hardcore is hidden for now (balance soft core first); it comes back later.
