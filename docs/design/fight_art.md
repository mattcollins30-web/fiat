# The fight's art (13 Oct, Matt; design, not built)

Matt: the art can take both the enemy's and the runner's boxes, as long as the health bars and the figures survive
somewhere; the search goes under it. That doubles the room: one scene, 588 x about 250, between the band and the rig.

Mockups (docs/design/mockups/fight_art_mockups.png, placeholders: the class bodies, the gang's darkened):
- **A, the stage.** One scene across both boxes - the site behind, the runner on the left, the gang on the right
  (as many figures as are on you, up to three). Their bar and the "on them" chips over the scene's top right, yours at
  its foot left; the figures in one row of six tiles under it (incoming, your fire, kills, armour, meds, rounds); the
  search bar under those while searching (the scene goes quiet: the site and the runner, no gang, "next gang 0:17").
- **B, the duel.** The scene the full height; each side's bar and figures in a dark wash over its own half. More
  picture, but the figures as words, smaller.

Leaning A: the tiles stay the size they are now and the bars read as they do; B is more picture but a squint.

What it needs: a figure per gang per zone (four zones, a few each - poses: standing, firing, down), the heads (one each,
the five), the runner per class (the bodies there are), the site's own backdrop (the band's art cropped, or a ground-level
plate per site). Hits as a flash on the figure, a kill as the figure dropping out. The art manifest after Matt picks.
