# Picked over, as the crates (and the Workshop to the Carpet Man)

Agreed with Matt on 27 Oct, from play ("I've just hit the wall"): the site wear thinned drops for two hours just when
the campaign asks for a grind. Parked for after the playtest; the canvas's "Picked over, as the crates".

## Picked over is the crates, across runs

- A site keeps its unopened crates between runs: their finds and the dog's nose come with them. The next run at the
  site picks up where the last left off. A signal's run at a site doesn't touch the campaign site's crates.
- The Map's site card lists what's left there ("Left here last run: Safe, Military crate, Toolbox, 2 boxes"), the
  searches left, and what the dog smelled.
- On the run, a click on a crate tile makes it the next: the current one finishes first; unpicked, the queue's order.
- Every crate open: picked clean. 20 minutes of play later the site restocks with a fresh roll.
- The wear goes: kill drops and cash on a cleared site stay whole (was drops -50%, cash -30%, back over RECOVER_H 2 h).
  With it go the old depth leftovers it still reads (worn_depth; `depth` is pinned at 1 in the campaign).
  The Atlas's "Picked over" rank (a site's own things more of its finds) is renamed so the term means one thing.
- Balance: more drops through the campaign - wants the post-playtest pass.

## The Workshop to the Carpet Man

- The Carpet Man opens the Workshop with a thing of his own, "The fitter's kit" (a carpet fitter's tools); the
  Quartermaster keeps the Radio. Before: the Carpet Man opened nothing, the Quartermaster two rooms.
- The radio's gate lines: a "before" for the Carpet Man's kit; the Quartermaster's "after" loses "I hear you've a
  workshop, too". The room cards, the gates' checks and the drawer's keepsake follow.
- Balance: the Workshop wants scrap and tins, which are thin early; the bot reaches it sooner.
