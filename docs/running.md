# Running things on Matt's machine: the full procedure

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 28 Oct (its one-screen table points here). Read the row for a job before dropping its flag.

The watcher (`tools\watch.bat`, a window Matt leaves open) runs jobs from flag files in `docs\balance\`:

| Job | Drop | Runs | Results | Done |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smoke | `smoke.flag` (empty) | `tools\smoke.bat` (~1.5 min) | `docs\balance\smoke.txt`; last line is the verdict | `smoke.done` (a timestamp) |
| Balance pass | `balance.flag` with the hours in it, e.g. `32` | `tools\balance.bat <hours>`: first the Radio's checkpoint (a new game to the Radio, `radio_checkpoint.save`), then side by side every element build as one save from the Poacher (its class taken when it opens) twice, every build 4 h from the checkpoint twice (`parity-<class>-<element>-N.txt`, `PARITY {json}`, judged on the mean; no repeat runs, and each stops itself at 50 min of wall under its 60-min kill, its line saying `cut_short` and `played_h`), then the gauntlet (26 Oct, Matt): each build's end state (`pass\<Class>-<element>-<N>.save`, one a read; the Nth gauntlet reads the Nth read's, the first's if it has none) through the endgame in order - the Grid, the Archive, the Mint, the County's twelve - three tries each, each try from whole (HP and wounds seen to) with the bot's three skills picked for the place, issued kit bought for each zone, a death or loss put back, the read stopping itself at 50 min of wall with the places left "cut short" (`gauntlet-<Class>-<element>-<N>.txt`, `GAUNTLET {json}`), and the six broken builds' pieces put on their class's end state the same way (`broken-<key>.txt`, `BROKEN {json}`; broken = every place won; outside the gates - its own table in the summary), the probe and the loot read. Each read runs through `tools\balance_read.bat <name> <minutes> <args>` (or `<name> <minutes> script <res path> <args>`; every argument after the minutes goes through, so a read by hand can end in `norepeat`; run_limited.ps1: killed past its minutes, or 15 min silent after a SCRIPT ERROR), and its `.done` says how it ended (`done ok`, `killed-time`, `killed-error`, `crashed`); `pass\expected.txt` lists every read started, `pass\deadline.log` marks the 5 h deadline firing; the summary names any read that didn't end ok | `docs\balance\pass\<build>-N.txt` (half-hour lines, purchases, a `GATES {json}` line; the folder is the pass's own, wiped at its start), the summary `docs\balance\summary.md` (each red with its kind: design, bot timing, bot play) | `balance.done`, each read `pass\<build>-N.done` |
| Quick pass (iterating) | `balance.flag` with `16 1 quick` | `tools\balance.bat 16 1 quick` (28 Oct, Matt: "buy time on balance passes"): the pass as above, but each one-save read gets `cap20` (tests/balance.gd stops it after 20 minutes of wall, its GATES line carrying `capped_at`, a CAPPED line in its .txt), each parity read the same 20 minutes (`cut_short` on its PARITY line), and there's no gauntlet; the summary's header names every capped read and how far it got (a "never" in a capped read may be the cap). The 28 Oct `24 1` pass took 1 h 38 min, nearly all of it the slowest one-save read | as the pass | `balance.done` |
| Long reads | `balance.flag` with `24 1 long` | `tools\balance.bat 24 1 long` (1 Nov, BUILD38: the full pass's reads stopped at two hours of wall, 4.6-13 game hours, too short for Retirement, the Issue, the County or the gauntlet's camp): the Radio's checkpoint (made fresh, as the pass makes it: pass\ is wiped first), then six one-save reads side by side and nothing else - Poacher as poison, Doorman as bleed, Paramedic as frost, Drifter as fire, Recruit as physical, Scrapper as shock - 24 game hours each with no two-hour stop (`-- 24 one <Class> <element> 0 long`; killed at 480 minutes of wall, each stopping itself at 440 so it still writes its GATES line, `capped_at` if it did). Each writes an `HOUR {json}` line every game hour (an hour one bot step passed along with the hour before is marked `same_step`: its state is that step's end, its window empty; the build's level, the reach, the paragon and its points unspent, cash held and earned that hour, XP that hour by source - signals, sites, Kev's story, grid / archive / mint, the County, repeats, expeditions, `lift` for the crew's floor - deaths, the Issue's hour, the County's places won) and its state at hour 20 (`long-<Class>-<element>-h20.save`, an `H20` line; at its end if it never got there; `snap=N` on the line moves the hour for a test). Then the gauntlet twice a build, side by side: from the end state with the standard camp, as the full pass's (`gauntlet-long-*`), and from the hour-20 state at the camp the six reads' hour 20 make - the median reach and paragon, the build's own kit and level (`gauntlet-h20-*`). Then the summary. Estimate on Matt's machine: up to about 10 h - the checkpoint a few minutes, the six reads as long as the slowest (likely 4-7.5 h, up to 8 h at the kill), the gauntlet up to 1 h | `docs\balance\pass\long-<Class>-<element>.txt` (HOUR, H20, GATES), `.save` and `-h20.save`; `gauntlet-long-*.txt`, `gauntlet-h20-*.txt`; the summary's "Long reads" section in `docs\balance\summary.md` (per build: level 50's hour against 12-24 h, the Issue, the County by hour 24, reach and cash at hours 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24, deaths after the Quartermaster; gate 8 judged on the 24 h reads; XP by source; "the standard camp, from the reads: reach N, paragon M"; the two gauntlets, G1 and G2) | `balance.done` |
| Broken builds alone | `balance.flag` with `0 0 broken` | `tools\balance.bat 0 0 broken`: the six broken builds' gauntlets from the last full pass's end states (refuses without them); the pass folder's other reads kept | `docs\balance\pass\broken-<key>.txt`, the table `docs\balance\broken.md` | `balance.done` (a bad flag: `docs\balance\flag_error.txt`, nothing run) |
| Build hunt | `balance.flag` with `2 0 hunt` | `tools\balance.bat 2 0 hunt` (26 Oct, Matt: "cook some different builds up"): from the pass's checkpoint at level 50, the runner staying out (20 patrols, the bail, or a short clock after the clear). Stage 1: every class's two joints, each with every set at its count or none, 2 h each in 12 shards (`pass\hunt-s1-<shard>.txt`, `HUNT {json}`). Stage 2: stage 1's best 20, each with every unique whose slot the set leaves free (`hunt-s2-*`). Then stage 2's best six through the gauntlet from the last pass's end states (`broken-hunt<n>`, `-- 0 broken hunt<n>`). Ranked by the damage really dealt a second fought; the finalists judged as the broken builds are | the tables `docs\balance\hunt.md` | `balance.done` |
| Playtest read | `playtest.flag` with a save path (or empty) | `tools\playtest.bat` | the playtest summary | `playtest.done` |

- From a cloud session the smoke is `bash $HOME/flaky/run.sh N` in the device shell: it drops the flag, waits for a
  fresh `smoke.done`, copies the log to `$HOME/flaky/smoke_N.txt` and greps `CHECK FAILED\|SCRIPT ERROR`. Check the
  done timestamp is new (it's Matt's local time: UK time, GMT in winter, BST in summer). The verdict is run.sh's own "run N (done ...)" line, never
  `smoke_N.txt` read on its own: the numbers wrap and an old run's file sits there (23 Oct: runs 61-63 read "green" off
  the 19th's files while the smoke hung). A run that writes no done file within run.sh's wait has hung: find where
  `docs/balance/smoke.txt` stopped, and the hung Godot has to be killed on Matt's machine before the next flag.
- The watcher runs one job at a time: no smoke while a balance pass runs.
- The one-save reads play the campaign as a crew (30 Oct, Matt: "walled with a class, you switch"): two hours of the yard's time with no step forward - a first clear or a new head taken at a person's odds (65%) or better - and the bot sends out the open class that reads the wall best: a played one at 65%, else a new one (which grows to the walled class's level before its own clock runs), else a played one reading it 5 points better (tests/bot.gd wall_check). The class walled is read again when it levels or every two hours and comes back when it reads 65% (wall_return); the read's own build isn't walled away while it's still levelling (a level in the last two hours), and comes back out when the campaign's done. A read that starts as its build (the Poacher's) holds it from the first step. Each read's walls are in GATES `walls` (`[hour, site, a head?, from, to, to's odds, from's odds]`) and the summary's Walls row. The end's figures (the odds, the gang clock, the level, the kit) are the read's build's: it's put back out before they're read (GATES `end_class` names the runner they read).
- The as-played reads (31 Oct, BUILD34): after the twelve, one a class (`<Class>-played-1`, `-- <hours> one <Class> played
  0 read1`), started and swapped as the crew read is, with the bot playing every class as the designer does
  (tests/bot.gd `as_played`): a class opens its two elements first, free (its build's first; a class swapped in
  closes the camp's it doesn't own), its class points go to both joints' class halves first and each open
  element's points to its joint half first, by the cheapest way in points (never through a keystone); the kit reads
  the second element at half. The quick pass caps them as the rest. Their GATES `played` (per class played: when each
  element opened, its points at the Quartermaster and the end, each joint's halves and closing hour and level, the
  Pitch it spent) feed the summary's own section, "As played: both elements", and its gate "Both joints" (design: both
  of the class's joints closed by level 30); every other gate and table leaves them out. `opened_how` says, per
  element, whether the class opened it itself (a Pitch point, or a free open of the camp's) or inherited it (the
  camp's two a class takes before it opens or closes one); each class is noted after every step, as it's swapped out,
  and once more when the read's build is put back at the end. An as-played read the pass started that left no GATES
  line is judged off ("no GATES line"), and the gate's row shows whenever the pass started any.
- A one-save read's build opens its element the step its class is taken (31 Oct, BUILD34: the Recruit as physical had
  played as the camp's frost and bleed until he out-levelled the crew; since the designer's call the same day a class's
  own two elements open free, so no Pitch point waits for it); GATES `el_open_at` is the hour it opened for its class.
- The bot never packs its own rig (30 Oct, the clean-up): it buys standard magazines for its guns' types (31 Oct, 0.10.2), boxes enough to fill them plus the gate's box for the refills, meds and cures into the stash, and the game's packer (`Sim.pack_rig` on "fill", whatever the save's top-up setting) puts them in (and `gate_box_always` packs the refill box on any save), so a read measures the rig a player gets on the default. balance.gd reads real magazines: no bottomless rounds.
- Two reads of a build are one row in the summary: the firsts by their median, the money by the mean, the counts summed - the walls joined, the repeats and the expeditions summed, the bases worn summed in hours, the damage by gun type each read's shares weighing the same. Gate 16 counts a build that never cleared a site as later than any (named); gate 15's scrap income includes the scrap shipped on expeditions.
- The gauntlet (26 Oct, in every full pass after the one-save reads): each build's end state
  (`pass/<Class>-<element>-<N>.save`) fights the Grid, the Archive, the Mint and the County's 12 in order, 3 tries each,
  the camp restored after every lost try; the six broken builds' pieces on too. The summary's `gauntlet_table`. Flags:
  `0 0 broken` (the broken builds' gauntlets alone, from the last pass's end states), `2 0 hunt` (the build hunt).
  A bad flag writes `docs\balance\flag_error.txt` and runs nothing.
- With the watcher off, a cloud session runs the full smoke in the cloud on a snapshot of its copy (xvfb, opengl3). Its
  known cloud-only reds are art the cloud copy lacks: gang faces, muzzle flashes, the Recruit's portraits, item
  pictures (a cell's words standing in, e.g. 'AMO'); every other red is real.
- The smoke's dice are fixed (seed 1025) so a red repeats; `-- seed N` runs it on another, `-- seed random` on a fresh
  one (its `SMOKE seed` line says which). `-- only <area>` runs one area.
- Long sim and balance runs are Matt's machine's (6 Oct: it's about twice as fast). One quick read between passes:
  `tools\read.bat <hours> <class> [element]`.
- gdlint shows 0 before any commit (`tools/gdlint.py`).
